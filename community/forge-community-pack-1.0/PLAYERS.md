# The Lantern Route — crew briefing

The navigation archive at ECHO RELAY has stopped reaching PORT LANTERN. Your Atlantis, **LANTERN-1**, must recover it and bring it home.

**Win:** deliver the archive through PORT LANTERN's conversation.
**Lose:** your ship, PORT LANTERN or ECHO RELAY is destroyed.

## Departure

Relay: hail **PORT LANTERN**, choose **Read mission orders**, then **Back to mission control**. When the captain is ready, choose **Open corridor to Echo Reach**. This mission uses a conversation to travel between sectors; using the ship's ordinary jump drive does not change sectors.

After any corridor transit, close the old comms window. Hail the station in the destination for the next step.

## At Echo Reach

Two hostile Kraylor Hornets guard the relay approach. They hold their positions and can fire when you enter their weapon arcs. Weapons: raise shields, select a hostile target and use beams or missiles. Helm: keep targets within the firing arcs and range; do not wait motionless for an enemy outside your arcs. Engineering: manage energy, shields and heat. Science: identify threats and help Helm maintain a useful position. Relay: track mission instructions in the ship log.

Protect the relay. Its conversation offers return transit if you need to regroup. An inactive sector freezes: returning does not grant a fresh set of enemies or reset their damage.

Once both raiders are gone, hail **ECHO RELAY** and select **Download the archive**. Follow its instructions to bring the archive home. Downloading alone does not win.

## Saving

With Campaign saves enabled, Engineering can request **Save checkpoint** before stopping. Checkpoints are also requested at transit and download. Resume on the same host installation and engine build. Treat a checkpoint as a limited campaign save, not an exact snapshot of every control: unload missiles before ending if you want to keep those rounds. Read the guide's save limitations.

Suggested crew: one shared ship with the normal EE stations. A solo player may switch between stations. No GM is needed. Allow roughly 15–25 minutes as a planning estimate, not a measured playtime or balance guarantee.
